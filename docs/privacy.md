# Reel Block privacy policy

Last updated: 19 November 2026. Applies to Reel Block 2.1 and later, from Google Play or GitHub.

## Short version

Reel Block collects no data. Nothing leaves your phone. The app has no internet permission, no account,
no ads, no analytics and no logs.

## What the app reads

Reel Block uses Android's accessibility service only in Instagram (`com.instagram.android`). It reads:

- that Instagram is open,
- the name (view ID) of the Instagram screen that is open, to see if it is the Reels viewer,
- which Instagram version is installed, to notice when an update breaks it.

When the Reels viewer is open, it presses Back. It never reads your messages, posts, photos, passwords or
any other app. Before it can do anything, the app shows this explanation and you must tap
**Agree and continue**.

## What the app keeps, only on your phone

- How many Reels it closed each day: for 30 days, or for 366 days with Pro. It also keeps the total.
  It keeps no time of day.
- Your pause setting, your Pro schedule, that you agreed to the explanation, and that the first run was shown.
- Whether Google Play says you bought Pro.

Android backup and phone-to-phone transfer copy none of this. Uninstalling the app deletes all of it.

## Purchases

Reel Block Pro is sold by Google Play. Google Play collects your payment details directly. Reel Block never
sees them. The app asks the Play Store app on your phone only whether Pro is bought, and it tells Google
Play that a purchase was received (Google requires this). The app sends nothing else, to Google or to anyone.
Google's privacy policy applies to the purchase: <https://policies.google.com/privacy>.

## Sharing, selling, children

Reel Block shares and sells no data, because it has none. It is not made for children.
